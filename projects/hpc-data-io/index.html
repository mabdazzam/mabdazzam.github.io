HPC I/O for deep-learning flood prediction

Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory · Manuscript in preparation

This study evaluates how storage formats and data-loading choices affect a geospatial flood-prediction workflow across three high-performance computing systems. It compares distinct hardware and system architectures, from CPU-based data reading through accelerator and distributed training.

The analysis focuses on how storage, data-loader configuration, and system architecture shape I/O and end-to-end training performance across workloads.
