Modeling the Mesilla–Conejos-Médanos aquifer

Ongoing application · ParFlow–CLM · TAAP

The Mesilla–Conejos-Médanos basin spans southern New Mexico, far west Texas, and northern Chihuahua. This project is developing an integrated ParFlow–CLM model to study water movement across the land surface and subsurface in a heavily managed, transboundary basin.

My work centers on building reproducible model inputs: preparing terrain, geology, soils, land cover, wells, and climate forcing; aligning those datasets to the computational grid; and testing the model workflow on workstation and HPC systems. The model is under development, so the figures here show study context and setup inputs rather than validated hydrologic findings.

Inputs are reprojected, aligned to a common grid and clock, checked for units and coverage, and prepared in formats suited to ParFlow’s parallel workflows. The broader TAAP project develops open, regional groundwater-modeling methods from public data sources.
