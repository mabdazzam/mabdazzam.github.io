GCN10: global 10 m Curve Number data

Dataset result · released

GCN10 is a high-resolution global Curve Number data product designed for runoff modeling and watershed analysis. It combines global land-cover and hydrologic-soil information to provide a finer spatial representation than widely used coarser products.
