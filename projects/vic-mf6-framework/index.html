VIC–MODFLOW 6 coupling framework

In development · coupled hydrologic modeling

I am developing an MPI-parallel framework that couples the Variable Infiltration Capacity (VIC) land-surface model with MODFLOW 6 for statewide, coupled surface–subsurface simulation in New Mexico. The models exchange water in both directions, allowing groundwater conditions to influence land-surface processes and surface fluxes to affect groundwater storage.

The technical work includes control of MODFLOW 6 through its API, mapping fluxes between grids with different cell boundaries, parallel execution, and reproducible model packaging. Verification focuses on exchange direction, water-budget closure, restart behavior, and consistent results across process counts.
