New Mexico statewide drought vulnerability analysis

Research reports · 2026

This work develops a physically based coupled hydrologic modeling framework to assess drought vulnerability across New Mexico under historical and future climate conditions.

My contributions included coupled model development, climate-data processing, distributed simulation workflows, and regional water-availability analysis.
