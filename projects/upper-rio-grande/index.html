Upper Rio Grande water and groundwater study

Exploratory analysis · 1980–2025

This regional application uses coupled land-surface and groundwater modeling to examine water storage, surface fluxes, and groundwater exchange across the Upper Rio Grande. The study is part of a broader effort to understand water availability and drought in a highly managed, water-limited basin.

The current run record covers 1980 through 2025. Completed years passed annual numerical review, while scientific calibration and drought-baseline acceptance remain ongoing. The figures below are exploratory model output, not calibrated projections.
