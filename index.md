# Muhammad Abdullah Azzam

Hydrologist and research software developer · Ph.D. researcher, HydroCS Lab, New Mexico State University

[Email](mailto:abdazzam@nmsu.edu) · [GitHub](https://github.com/mabdazzam) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BKSsRsEAAAAJ) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mabdazzam) · [CV](assets/cv.pdf)

My work connects computational hydrology with hydraulic and flood-inundation modeling. I develop process-based models, run large simulations and data workflows on high-performance computing systems, and use hydrologic and terrain information to study runoff, drought, groundwater, and flood hazards. A recurring thread is connecting large-scale hydrologic simulations with hydraulic flood-risk analysis.

My experience aligns with two connected areas:

- **Computational hydrology:** process-based model development and coupling; regional to continental simulations; historical and projected climate forcing; parallel computing and large geospatial data.
- **Hydraulic and flood-inundation modeling:** physically based 1D/2D hydraulic analysis, flood mapping and risk assessment, terrain-aware workflows, and large-domain flood modeling on HPC systems.

## Research & current work

- [VIC–MODFLOW 6 coupled modeling framework](prototype/projects/vic-mf6-framework/): MPI-parallel, two-way model coupling; framework manuscript and regional validation are in progress.
- [Upper Rio Grande groundwater and drought study](prototype/projects/upper-rio-grande/): regional coupled-model analysis for 1980–2025; current results are exploratory.
- [CONUS-scale coupling pilot](prototype/projects/conus-coupling/): January 2010 run completed; hydrologic validation remains underway.
- [New Mexico statewide drought vulnerability](prototype/projects/nm-drought/): coupled-model development, climate processing, and distributed analysis; documented in NMWRRI Reports 412 and 413.
- [Mesilla–Conejos-Médanos aquifer modeling](prototype/projects/mesilla-aquifer/): ParFlow–CLM setup and reproducible data workflows for a transboundary basin.
- [TORRENT flood modeling on HPC systems](prototype/projects/torrent-flood/): large-domain inundation modeling and workflow development.
- [Applied hydraulic and flood studies](prototype/projects/hydraulic-flood/): 1D/2D lake-plain modeling, climate-scenario flood-risk assessment, and stream design studies.
