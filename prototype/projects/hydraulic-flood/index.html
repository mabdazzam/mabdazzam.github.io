Hydraulic and flood-inundation studies

Applied water-resources engineering · hydrology and hydraulics

Before beginning my Ph.D., I worked on applied hydrologic and hydraulic studies for flood-risk, water-supply, drainage, and infrastructure projects. This work connected rainfall-runoff analysis, hydraulic modeling, terrain and spatial data, and flood-impact assessment.

Selected applications

  • Flood inundation in the Manchar and Hamal Lake plains: completed 1D/2D hydraulic modeling and compared inundation patterns with Google Earth Engine maps for historical and projected climate-change events.
  • Indus River Basin flood-risk assessment: contributed to flood-risk and impact assessment under climate-change scenarios across parts of Pakistan, including Punjab.
  • Karachi Bulk Water Supply Scheme IV: studied rainfall-runoff response for 82 major streams and rivers along the pipeline alignment, developed design flood hydrographs, and assessed stream-bed erosion and sediment transport.

Connection to current research

My work extends this foundation through scalable hydrologic simulation and the large-domain TORRENT modeling I undertook during my internship at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. A manuscript on the TORRENT work is in preparation.

Modeling tools in my background include HEC-HMS, HEC-RAS, TRITON, and TORRENT; computational work includes C, Python, MPI, Linux, and HPC systems.
