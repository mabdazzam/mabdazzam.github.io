CONUS-scale coupled modeling pilot

Early application · January 2010

This pilot applies the VIC–MODFLOW 6 framework to a continental-scale model domain. The completed January 2010 simulation advanced through 31 daily coupling windows and passed framework numerical checks, including exchange mapping and domain-level water-budget closure.

Hydrologic validation is still pending, and the current exchange settings are provisional. I present this as an early scale demonstration while physical review and additional checks continue.
